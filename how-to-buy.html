5 Easy Steps to Buy Crypto

Follow these steps and you'll be holding crypto in no time

1

Choose an Exchange

Pick a trusted platform. For Nigerians I recommend Binance, Bybit, or local P2P options like Quidax and Busha.

See all platforms →
2

Create an Account

Sign up with email or phone number. Use a strong password and enable 2FA immediately.

3

Verify Your Identity (KYC)

Upload your ID or NIN. Most platforms approve Nigerians within 10-30 minutes.

4

Deposit Funds

Fund your account with Naira via bank transfer, Flutterwave, or P2P. No need for USD.

5

Buy Your First Crypto

Search for BTC, ETH, USDT or any coin you want, then click "Buy".

Safety Checklist Before You Buy

  • Enable 2FA

  • Never share your seed phrase

    Not even with customer support.

  • Withdraw to your own wallet

    Don’t leave large amounts on exchanges.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much money do I need to start?

You can start with as little as ₦5,000. Many Nigerians begin with ₦10,000–₦50,000.

Can I buy crypto with Naira directly?

Yes! Binance P2P, Bybit, Quidax, and Busha all allow direct Naira deposits and withdrawals.

Is it safe to buy crypto in Nigeria?

Yes, as long as you use reputable platforms and follow the safety tips above.

Do I need to pay tax on crypto profits in Nigeria?

Currently there is no specific crypto tax law, but FIRS treats crypto gains as capital gains. Always keep records.
